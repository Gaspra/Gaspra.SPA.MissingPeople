MISSING PEOPLE

OPEN DECKS SIGN UP

Date: Every 3rd Sunday

Time: 1pm - 7pm

Location: Dead Wax Social


20 minute sets (open music policy)

Equipment provided (turntables, mixer & linked CDJs)



Use this form to get in touch for more info about Missing People and to book a set at our next Open Decks. Please tell us your name and preferred time you’d like to play* and we’ll get back to you asap with confirmation.

The event runs from 1-7pm on the 3rd Sunday of each month. Sets are 20 mins, with no music policy. We are an open event in every sense of the word and are always ready to hear new musical styles from any genre. Equipment is provided, we have CDJs for digital and turntables for vinyl DJs. Please bring your own headphones if possible. *Please note that demand for these events is high, space is extremely limited and while we try to accommodate everyone, submission of a request does not automatically guarantee a set. If we are unable to book you in for any reason, please feel welcome to come along anyway, say hi, hang out and get the vibe of the event. We’re a welcoming bunch and always excited to meet new folks. Bring your friends but don’t bring any neg vibes, we won’t tolerate racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, xenophobia or any form of discrimination at our events ✌️
